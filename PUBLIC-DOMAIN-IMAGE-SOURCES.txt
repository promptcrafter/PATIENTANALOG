HOW TO VERIFY LICENSE
======================

PubMed Central (PMC):
1. Open article
2. Scroll to bottom
3. Look for "License" or "Copyright"
4. CC BY 4.0 = ✅ FREE TO USE
5. CC BY-NC = ❌ No commercial use (avoid)
6. "All rights reserved" = ❌ No use

NASA Images:
- All public domain unless stated otherwise
- No attribution required (but recommended)

FDA/NIH/CDC/DARPA:
- Government work = public domain
- No permission needed
- No attribution required (but recommended)

<!-- Image source: NASA Image Library, [Mission/ID], Public Domain -->
