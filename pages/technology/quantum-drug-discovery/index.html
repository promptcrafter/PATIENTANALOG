MARKET OVERVIEW

Quantum computing promises to revolutionize drug discovery by enabling molecular simulations impossible on classical computers. McKinsey projects quantum applications in pharma could generate $200-500 billion in value by 2035, with near-term utility expected between 2028-2035 as error-corrected quantum systems mature.

PHARMA PARTNERSHIPS

  • IBM Quantum Network: Merck, Biogen, Boehringer Ingelheim partnerships
  • Google Quantum AI: Collaboration with Boehringer for molecular simulation
  • IonQ: Partnerships with Dow, Hyundai for molecular modeling
  • D-Wave: Optimization problems in clinical trial design

KEY APPLICATIONS

  • Protein Folding: Quantum simulation of protein conformations
  • Molecular Dynamics: Electronic structure calculations for drug-target interactions
  • Virtual Screening: Quantum-enhanced library screening
  • ADME Prediction: More accurate pharmacokinetic modeling

TIMELINE TO UTILITY

  • 2024-2027: NISQ era - hybrid quantum-classical algorithms
  • 2028-2030: Early fault-tolerant systems for small molecules
  • 2030-2035: Full utility for complex biological simulations
