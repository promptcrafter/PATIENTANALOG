Sensor Technology

Biosensor Integration

Real-Time Physiological Monitoring

← Back to Science

Integrated biosensors enable continuous, non-destructive monitoring of organ-chip function. Technologies include TEER for barrier integrity, MEA for electrophysiology, oxygen sensors, pH monitoring, and metabolite detection→providing real-time readouts of tissue health and drug response.

SENSOR TYPES

TEER
Barrier Integrity

Trans-epithelial electrical resistance measures tight junction integrity in gut, lung, and BBB models.

MEA
Electrophysiology

Multi-electrode arrays record cardiac and neural electrical activity for arrhythmia and seizure detection.

OXYGEN
Metabolic Activity

Dissolved oxygen sensors monitor cellular respiration and metabolic status in real-time.

BIOMARKERS
Secreted Factors

Immunosensors detect albumin, urea, cytokines, and other secreted biomarkers continuously.
