COMMERCIAL ORGAN-ON-CHIP MARKET

Leading companies Emulate Inc. (US), Mimetas B.V. (Netherlands), and CN Bio Innovations Ltd (UK) offer commercial organ-on-chip platforms simulating human organ functions. These companies license hardware under multi-year contracts, bundle cloud analytics for recurring revenue, and provide pre-seeded and custom organ models. The market faces growth challenges from high system costs but is expanding through pharma partnerships and regulatory acceptance pathways.

MAJOR COMMERCIAL PLATFORMS

EMULATE, INC. (US)
Liver-Chip S1 - FDA ISTAND Accepted

Emulate's Liver-Chip S1 was accepted into FDA's ISTAND Pilot Program (Sept 2024)—the first organ-on-chip to reach this milestone. Demonstrated 87% sensitivity and 100% specificity for DILI prediction, outperforming animal models. The company licenses hardware under multi-year contracts with cloud analytics. Total funding: $225M including $82M Series E led by Northpond Ventures.

MIMETAS B.V. (NETHERLANDS)
OrganoPlate & OrganoReady Platform

Major products include OrganoReady (pre-seeded organ-on-chip platform) and OrganoPlates (microfluidic devices for culturing organ cells in controlled micro-environments). In August 2024, MIMETAS expanded OrganoPlate technology to include a kidney-on-a-chip model. Provides pre-seeded and non-seeded models for drug and chemical testing.

CN BIO INNOVATIONS LTD (UK)
PhysioMimix Platform - Gut/Liver Integration

CN Bio partnered with Altis Biosystems to offer Gut/Liver-on-a-chip model for assessing ADME properties and oral bioavailability. PhysioMimix Bioavailability assay kit launched November 2024. PhysioMimix Core commercially available. Secured $21M Series B funding (April 2024) led by Bayland Capital to expand product development and scaling.

TISSUSE GMBH (GERMANY)
Multi-Organ-Chip Platform

TissUse develops multi-organ-chip systems connecting multiple organ models to simulate systemic drug distribution and metabolism. Platforms enable assessment of multi-organ toxicity, drug-drug interactions, and pharmacokinetic profiles in human-relevant models.

INSPHERO (SWITZERLAND)
3D InSight Liver & Tumor Models

InSphero provides 3D InSight human tissue microtissues for toxicology and efficacy testing. Products include liver microtissues for hepatotoxicity assessment, tumor microtissues for oncology drug screening, and pancreatic islets for diabetes research. Offers GLP-compatible protocols and standardized assay kits.

ORGANOVO HOLDINGS, INC. (US)
ExVive 3D Bioprinted Tissues

Organovo produces bioprinted 3D human tissues including ExVive liver and kidney tissues. These functional tissue models maintain viability for extended culture periods, enabling chronic toxicity studies and drug metabolism assessments not possible with traditional cell cultures.

2024-2025 PRODUCT LAUNCHES

Aug 2024
Mimetas Kidney-on-Chip

MIMETAS B.V. expanded OrganoPlate technology to include kidney-on-a-chip model for nephrotoxicity assessment and renal drug transport studies, complementing existing liver, gut, and vascular models.

Nov 2024
CN Bio Bioavailability Kit

CN Bio launched PhysioMimix Bioavailability assay kit integrating Gut/Liver-on-a-chip to assess oral bioavailability and first-pass metabolism, addressing critical ADME questions in drug development.

Sept 2024
Emulate FDA ISTAND

Emulate's Liver-Chip S1 accepted into FDA ISTAND Pilot Program—first organ-on-chip to achieve this regulatory milestone, validating organ-chip technology for IND submissions and regulatory decision-making.

Jan 2024
Corning-Emulate Partnership

Corning announced strategic collaboration with Emulate to co-develop integrated organ-on-chip workflows, combining Corning's device materials with Emulate's technology for industrial-scale organ model deployment.

COMMERCIAL PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Platforms

Hardware Systems

Multi-year license contracts for microfluidic devices, perfusion systems, and incubation platforms. Includes cloud-connected analytics, automated imaging, and real-time biosensor monitoring capabilities.

Models

Pre-Seeded Organ Chips

OrganoReady platforms provide pre-seeded, quality-controlled organ models (liver, kidney, gut, lung, heart) ready for immediate compound testing. Reduces setup time and improves reproducibility across experiments.

Assay Kits

Application-Specific Kits

Bioavailability assay kits (CN Bio), DILI assessment protocols (Emulate), nephrotoxicity screening (Mimetas). GLP-compatible protocols with standardized endpoints for regulatory submissions.

Business Model

Subscription Revenue

Companies license hardware under multi-year contracts and bundle cloud analytics for recurring subscription revenue. Consumables (media, chips, cells) provide additional revenue streams with high customer lock-in.

Challenge

High System Costs

High costs associated with organ-on-chip systems may hamper market growth. Companies are addressing through automation, standardized protocols, and pharma partnerships to demonstrate ROI versus animal testing.

Expansion

Series B Funding & Scaling

CN Bio ($21M Series B, April 2024) and other companies securing growth capital for product expansion, manufacturing scale-up, and commercial team building to address pharmaceutical demand.
