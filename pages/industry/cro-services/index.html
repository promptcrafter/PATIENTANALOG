PRECLINICAL CRO MARKET OVERVIEW

The preclinical CRO market is valued at $7.63 billion in 2025, projected to reach $10.92 billion by 2030, growing at 8.73% CAGR. Major players including Charles River Laboratories, Eurofins Scientific, WuXi AppTec, and ICON are integrating organ-on-chip and organoid technologies into service offerings. Patient-derived organoid (PDO) models represent the fastest-growing segment at 7.54% CAGR, offering higher physiological relevance than traditional 2D cultures.

LEADING CRO PROVIDERS

CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES
Biologics & Vector Capabilities

Charles River completed strategic acquisitions to broaden biologics capabilities, including the Vigene acquisition. In June 2023, partnered with Curigin to enhance adenoviral vector production. The company offers GLP-compliant preclinical and clinical trial services integrating organ-on-chip platforms for ADME-Tox assessment.

EUROFINS SCIENTIFIC
RNA Therapeutics & ADME-Tox Platform

In November 2024, Eurofins announced strategic partnerships with biopharma firms for GLP-compliant toxicology support for RNA-based therapeutics. In February 2024, launched a high-throughput ADME-Tox screening platform integrating organ-on-chip technologies to accelerate early-stage drug safety assessments.

INOTIV (BIOANALYTICAL SYSTEMS)
Organoid Research Facility (Jan 2025)

In January 2025, Inotiv opened a new organoid research facility in Indiana, supporting immuno-oncology and neurodegenerative drug pipelines with customizable human tissue models. The facility offers patient-derived organoids (PDOs) for personalized medicine applications and high-throughput drug screening.

WUXI APPTEC
Integrated Drug Development Services

WuXi AppTec commands substantial preclinical CRO market share through comprehensive service portfolios. The company integrates organ-on-chip validation into small molecule and biologics development pipelines, offering end-to-end services from target identification through IND-enabling studies.

ICON PLC
Clinical & Preclinical Integration

ICON provides integrated preclinical and clinical development services. The company is adopting microphysiological systems (MPS) and organoid platforms to bridge preclinical findings with clinical outcomes, improving translational predictability for sponsor companies.

LABCORP DRUG DEVELOPMENT
Central Lab & Bioanalytical Services

Labcorp offers comprehensive preclinical services including bioanalytical testing, toxicology, and pharmacokinetics. The company is integrating organ-on-chip and 3D tissue models into safety assessment protocols to enhance human-relevant toxicity predictions.

ORGANOID & MPS SERVICE CATEGORIES

PDO Models
Patient-Derived Organoids

PDO models held the largest market share in 2024, replicating 3D structure, cellular diversity, and genetic profiles of patient tissue. Growing at 7.54% CAGR, offering higher physiological relevance for personalized medicine and drug response testing.

MPS Systems
Microphysiological Systems

Organ-on-chip and microphysiological systems improve translational predictability. CROs are deploying automated MPS platforms for high-throughput ADME-Tox screening, cardiotoxicity assessment, and hepatotoxicity prediction.

3D Tissue
3D Tissue Constructs

Advanced in vitro systems including 3D tissue constructs and microfluidic chips offer higher physiological relevance compared to traditional 2D cultures. CROs are integrating these platforms for oncology, neuroscience, and infectious disease research.

AI Integration
AI & Machine Learning

CROs are investing in AI-enabled ADME-Tox prediction platforms and integrated data systems. Machine learning models trained on organ-on-chip data improve drug development efficiency and reduce animal testing requirements.

CROs are integrating AI-enabled ADME-Tox prediction, organ-on-chip validation, and cloud analytics platforms. Automated MPS systems enable high-throughput screening while maintaining physiological relevance.
