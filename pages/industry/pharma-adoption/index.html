REGULATORY INFLECTION POINT

On September 24, 2024, the FDA accepted the first organ-on-chip technology into its ISTAND Pilot Program—Emulate's Liver-Chip for drug-induced liver injury (DILI) assessment. This milestone marked an unmistakable inflection point: pharmaceutical sponsors are no longer merely allowed to submit New Approach Methodologies (NAMs); they are increasingly expected to do so. The acceptance demonstrated 87% sensitivity and 100% specificity, outperforming animal models.

MAJOR PHARMACEUTICAL PARTNERSHIPS

ROCHE
Institute of Human Biology (IHB)

Roche established the IHB to develop drug discovery approaches using human model systems including organoids. One antibody progressed from concept to Phase 3 clinical trials in 2.5 years based on organoid testing alone— no animal models or cell lines involved—resulting in a safe, effective molecule now in late-stage trials.

EMULATE PARTNERSHIPS
Corning & AstraZeneca Collaborations

Corning announced (January 2024) a strategic collaboration with Emulate to co-develop integrated organ-on-chip workflows for industrial-scale organ models. AstraZeneca partners with Emulate to deploy organ-on-chip technology across R&D pipelines for improved predictive toxicology and efficacy testing.

ASTRAZENECA
Academic & Technology Partnerships

AstraZeneca collaborates with academic institutions to develop organ-on-chip technologies and advanced in vitro models. The company is investing in AI and machine learning integration to predict drug toxicity and efficacy, reducing reliance on animal testing in preclinical development.

GLAXOSMITHKLINE (GSK)
Non-Animal Technology Investment

GSK works with organizations supporting non-animal technologies including EUROoCS, the Microphysiological Systems Initiative of NA3RsC, and NC3Rs. The company is integrating MPS platforms into safety assessment workflows to improve human-relevant toxicity predictions.

SANOFI
AI-Driven Predictive Models

Sanofi is investing in artificial intelligence and machine learning to predict drug toxicity and efficacy using in silico models combined with organ-on-chip validation. This integrated approach aims to accelerate candidate selection while reducing animal testing requirements.

MERCK KGAA
HUB Organoids Acquisition (Dec 2024)

Merck KGaA acquired HUB Organoids B.V. in December 2024, signaling that major pharmaceutical suppliers are betting on organoids becoming standard tools rather than niche research products. This acquisition strengthens Merck's portfolio in patient-derived models for oncology and precision medicine applications.

KEY ADOPTION DRIVERS

2024-2025
Regulatory Inflection Point

FDA ISTAND acceptance (Sept 2024) and FDA Modernization Act 2.0 (2023) eliminated animal testing requirements, creating regulatory pull for NAMs adoption across pharma R&D.

40%
CAGR - Organ-on-Chip Market

Organ-on-chip represents the fastest-growing segment in human simulation technologies, driven by pharmaceutical demand for predictive ADME-Tox platforms and regulatory acceptance pathways.

87%+
Predictive Accuracy

Tumor organoids demonstrate 87%+ drug response accuracy compared to patient outcomes. Emulate's Liver-Chip showed 87% sensitivity and 100% specificity for DILI prediction, outperforming animal models.

$225M
Emulate Total Funding

Emulate has raised $225M including an $82M Series E round led by Northpond Ventures, demonstrating investor confidence in commercial organ-on-chip platforms for pharmaceutical applications.

CROSS-PHARMA COLLABORATION

IQ MPS Affiliate Consortium

The IQ MPS Affiliate (established 2018, originally formed 2014) operates under the International Consortium for Innovation and Quality in Pharmaceutical Development. This cross-pharma consortium connects thought leaders, provides forums for collaboration and data sharing, and engages with regulators to qualify translationally relevant MPS models. The group has published multiple surveys (2024-2025) on industry adoption status and standardization requirements for microphysiological systems.

MARKET OUTLOOK

Trend

Regulatory Expectations

Following FDA ISTAND acceptance and FDA Modernization Act 2.0, pharmaceutical companies face increasing regulatory expectations to incorporate NAMs including organ-on-chip and organoid data in IND submissions.

Trend

Internal Capabilities

Major pharma companies (Roche IHB, AstraZeneca, GSK) are building internal MPS capabilities while simultaneously partnering with technology providers (Emulate, CN Bio, Mimetas) for specialized platforms and expertise.

Trend

M&A Activity

Merck's acquisition of HUB Organoids (Dec 2024) signals industry consolidation and mainstream adoption. Expect continued M&A as pharma companies acquire organoid and organ-on-chip platform capabilities.
