Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 14, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Patient Analog uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you use our platform
  • Improve our content and user experience
  • Measure the effectiveness of our content
  • Provide analytics and performance data

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for basic site functionality Session
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (Google Analytics) 2 years
Preferences Remember your settings like language and theme preferences 1 year
Performance Help us improve site speed and performance Session

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): For website analytics and understanding user behavior. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: For loading web fonts. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: For content delivery and security. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking all cookies may affect site functionality.
  • Google Analytics Opt-out: You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Do Not Track: We respect Do Not Track browser settings.

6. Browser-Specific Instructions

To manage cookies in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
